# Running

Karlova Ves is an excellent place for runners, offering a wide variety of options for track running, road running, and trail running.

## Track Running

Several athletics tracks are available within or near Karlova Ves:

- **Tilgnerova High School** (Segnerova tram stop, lines 4 and 9).
- **Majerníkova High School** in Dlhé Diely (Základná škola Majerníkova bus stop, lines 32 and 33).
- A smaller and often crowded oval can be found near the **Park Suchá Vydrica** in the Riviera area.
- One of the most accessible options is the track at the **Faculty of Physical Education and Sports**, located next to the Lanfranconi Bridge and easily reachable by tram lines 4 and 9.

## Road Running

Karlova Ves is well connected to both the Danube promenade and the riverside paths on the opposite bank. Together, they provide many kilometers of uninterrupted running routes along the river. The route on the southern bank forms part of the **EuroVelo 6 (EV6)** international cycling route.

Running to Devín via Devínska cesta is technically possible, but the road does not provide a safe running environment due to vehicle traffic.

For runners looking for elevation gain, there are two popular options:

1. Start in the Riviera area, run uphill to **Dlhé Diely** (final stop of bus lines 32 and 33), and continue along the road leading toward the forest and **Kráľova hora**.
2. A steeper alternative starts at **Zlaté schody**, continues via **Dlhé Diely I**, passes the final bus stop, and enters the forest. This route gains nearly 200 meters in elevation.

For shorter and flatter runs, **SNP Park** in **Líščie údolie** is a popular choice.

## Trail Running

Karlova Ves offers direct access to some of the best trail-running terrain in Bratislava.

For shorter runs, the trails around **Kráľova hora** provide numerous loop options and scenic views throughout the year. The network of paths allows runners to adjust both distance and difficulty.

For longer runs, popular destinations include:

- **Jezuitské lesy**
- **Dúbravská hlavica**
- **Devínska Kobyla**
- **Sandberg**
- **Devín**

Many runners combine these locations into longer loops before returning to Karlova Ves. An alternative is to continue to Dúbravka and return by tram.

Routes extending beyond Dúbravská hlavica toward Devínska Kobyla, Devín, or Sandberg offer beautiful views and can easily reach distances of 15 to 20 kilometers. For even longer adventures, the trail network connects to the wider **Malé Karpaty** mountain range.

## Running Events

Several well-known running events pass through or near Karlova Ves:

### Devín – Bratislava Run

The most famous is the **Devín – Bratislava Run**, an 11.2 km road race with a tradition spanning more than 90 years. It starts beneath Devín Castle and finishes in central Bratislava.

### Štefánik Trail

The **Štefánik Trail** passes through **Líščie údolie**. This 55 km trail race can be completed individually or as part of a relay team. The race starts in Devín and finishes in the Old Town.

### Beh Devínskou Kobylou

The third event is **Beh Devínskou Kobylou**, a shorter trail-running race with a tradition of more than 50 years. It takes runners through some of the most attractive forest trails in western Bratislava.
