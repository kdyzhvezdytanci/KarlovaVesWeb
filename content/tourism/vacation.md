What to do in Karlova ves

The original idea on this website was to create website that would promote Karlova Ves as a tourist locations equally to some established world top destination such as maledives, dubai or new york. It grew my mind to become more of a attemt to serve small comunity of people coming to Karlova Ves. But here I am paying the tribute to my original idea placing my home neighberhhod among giants like London or Barcelona.

Airway to Karlova ves is operated by two airpots Letisko Milana Rastislava Stefanika and Schwechat airport. Upon landing and transport you are free to choose between a hotel or private residences via services like air bnb. Former being more suitable for young people and later for families.
