#**Transport in Bratislava**
Karlova Ves enjoys a diverse and efficient transport system that combines trams, buses, road infrastructure, cycling routes, and pedestrian-friendly (more so if you enjoy hills) urban design. 
Its excellent connections to central Bratislava, educational and research hubs in Mlynská dolina, and recreational areas along the Danube make it one of the most accessible and convenient districts in the Slovak capital. 
The balance between urban mobility and access to nature remains one of Karlova Ves's greatest advantages.
It also have the advantage of being in a relatively middle sized. And although there is no metro in Bratislava vast majority of bratislava is accessible directly or with one change.

#**Public transport** (called MHD, abr. from Mestska hromadna doprava)

##**Trams**

**Tram 4** - Starts the beggining of Dubravka, and passing through Karlova ves Stare and nove mesto reaches Zlate piesky. Recreational area at the end of bratislava.
Directly accessible spots: Zlate Piesky, Hockey, Footbll and tenis national stadiums. Novy most and its lines

**Tram 9** - Starts at Kutiki, which is first tram stop from Dubravka direction and goes via tunnel under Bratislava Castle hill, throgh old town and ends in ruzinov.
Directly accessible spots: Trnavske Myto, Mileticova market, Hospital at Ruzinov. Airport.

##**Buses**

**Line 32** connects Karlova ves with kramare, one of bratislavas major hospitals and ends at Bratislava Main train station.

**Line 33** operates on Dlhe diely. Connecting tram network with Residential areas on the hill, with end stop just before ascend forest above karlova ves.

**Line 137** goes to Stare grunty and Mynska dolina. Accessing Cubicon shopping centre, Slaviecie udilie cementary and Student camuses. Tough more convenient is often connection with tram and bus 31 or 33. 137 has longer intervals and services residential areas as well.

**Line 35** goes to Dubravka from the end stop of lines 32 and 33. It is a supplementary service, that goes less often and operated by smaller bus.

##**Bicycle**

**Nábrežná cyklotrasa** (popri Dunaji): Hlavná tepna vedúca z Karlovej Vsi smerom do centra mesta (popri Botanickej záhrade a nábreží) a opačným smerom na Devín.
**Karloveské Líščie údolie:** Rekreačná trasa a prepojenie do prírodného prostredia, kde sa diskutuje o úprave na bezpečnejšiu bicyklovú cestu.
**Spojenia do Dúbravky a Lamača:** V okolí sa nachádzajú nadväzujúce trasy a radiály (napr. smerom na Dúbravskú radiálu)
